PRISM stats — last updated November 18, 2026

PRISM stats is a private analytics tool used by the owner of the PRISM channel («Мир под другим углом») on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It reads view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own published videos so the owner can compare how the videos perform. It is not offered to other users.
