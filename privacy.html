Privacy Policy

PRISM stats — last updated November 18, 2026

PRISM stats is used only by the owner of the PRISM channel and only for the owner's own accounts.

What is accessed: basic profile information of the owner's account (name, follower and video counts) and statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, average watch time).

What is not accessed: data of other users, private messages, contacts or anything beyond the permissions granted at sign-in.

Storage: the numbers and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the settings of YouTube (Google account permissions), Instagram/Meta and TikTok; the locally stored data is then deleted by the owner.

Contact: via Instagram @prism.world.
